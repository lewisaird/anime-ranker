Community rules

Last updated: October 2026

These rules apply to Kessen's shared features: share links, Watch Together and Live Challenge. They apply when you create a share link, or create or join a session.

What others can see

The name shown for you, the anime you rank, nominate or pick, and your votes, picks and predictions. There is no chat.

Not allowed, for anyone

  • sexual or pornographic content, and anything that sexualises children;
  • encouraging suicide, self-harm or eating disorders;
  • bullying, harassment or threats, and hate or abuse aimed at anyone, including for their race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability or gender identity;
  • violent content, or encouraging dangerous stunts or harmful substances;
  • anything illegal;
  • pretending to be someone else;
  • names or usernames that break these rules;
  • trying to break or misuse Kessen.

Reporting

Tap Report this page on a share page, or Report this session in Watch Together or Live Challenge, or email feedback@kessen.co.uk with the link or session code and what is wrong. We aim to look at every report within 7 days, and sooner for anything illegal or that could harm a child. We remove what breaks these rules, for example by deleting the share link or session.

Complaints and appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, email feedback@kessen.co.uk and we will look again and reply. See also the privacy policy.
